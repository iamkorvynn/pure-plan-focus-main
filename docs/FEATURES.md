# ✨ Feature Breakdown

This document provides a detailed overview of the application's core functionality, organized by module.

---

## 🛠️ Dashboard

The dashboard is the central hub for your productivity. It includes several key components that provide a quick overview of your day.

- **Hero Banner**: A dynamic greeting and daily focus message.
- **User Header**: Navigation, profile settings, and daily stats.
- **Category Cards**: Quick access to different task categories (Work, Life, Health, etc.).

---

## ✅ Task Management

The **Task Manager** is the heart of the application. It allows you to:

- Create, edit, and delete tasks.
- Assign categories and priorities.
- Set due dates for each task.
- Filter and prioritize tasks based on their status or category.
- Mark tasks as completed to track your progress throughout the day.

---

## 📅 Calendar & Scheduling

The **Calendar View** provides a visual representation of your schedule:

- Displays all tasks based on their due dates.
- Interactive calendar grid for navigating through different months.
- Integrates with the **Upcoming Tasks** widget for a compact, chronological view of your next immediate priorities.

---

## 💪 Health & Fitness

Pure-Plan-Focus helps you maintain a healthy lifestyle with dedicated modules:

- **Workouts**: Plan and track your exercise routines. Support for different types (Strength, Cardio, Mobility, etc.) and intensity levels. Includes detailed exercise lists (reps, sets, sets) and duration.
- **Meal Planning**: Track your daily nutrition with breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snack entries. Helps you stay on top of your dietary goals.
- **Habit Tracking**: A visual habit loop to maintain your daily streaks. Set daily recurring habits and mark them as done each day.

---

## 🧩 Interactive Widgets

Several interactive widgets are integrated into the dashboard to enhance your focus and daily routine:

- **Spotify Widget**: Control your focus music directly from the dashboard. Connect to your Spotify account to see currently playing tracks and playlists.
- **Upcoming Tasks**: A chronological list of your next immediate tasks, displayed in a compact sidebar format.
- **Journal/Mood Tracking**: (Implied by `JournalContext`) A module for daily reflections and mood monitoring.

---

## 💰 Finance Tracking

Monitor your financial health with basic income and expense tracking:

- Log daily expenses and income.
- Categorize financial entries.
- View a summary of your financial status (income vs. expenses).
